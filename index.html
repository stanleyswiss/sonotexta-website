How it works

Three seconds from thought to text.

  1. 1

    Press the Globe key

    Tap to start and stop, or hold to talk. Any shortcut works.

  2. 2

    Just talk

    A small pill shows the live waveform and transcript while you speak.

  3. 3

    Text lands where you type

    Cleaned up, translated or reformatted — pasted straight into the app you're in.
