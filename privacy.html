Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Sonotexta is a free, open-source dictation app for macOS, built by Stanislav Ivanov (sonotexta.com). This page explains what the app and this website do with your data. Because the code is public, you can check every claim here in the source.

The short version: there is no account, no telemetry and no analytics. Your audio and text never pass through a server of mine. They leave your Mac only if you choose a cloud speech engine or AI provider, and then they go straight from your Mac to that provider.

1. Audio

Sonotexta uses your microphone only to turn your speech into text, and only while you are dictating. How the audio is processed depends on the engine you pick:

  • On-device engines (Apple Speech, Whisper, Parakeet, Parakeet Live, Qwen3-ASR) run entirely on your Mac and work offline. The audio never leaves your device.
  • Cloud engines (OpenAI Realtime, Deepgram, ElevenLabs) receive your audio directly from your Mac, using your own API key and under that provider's terms and privacy policy.

Sonotexta does not save your audio. Once a dictation is transcribed, the audio buffer is discarded.

2. Text, AI cleanup and translation

If you turn on AI cleanup, translation or tone adjustment, the transcribed text (not the audio) is sent to the provider you selected, using your own API key. Supported providers include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama Cloud and DeepL. Ollama running on your own machine and Apple Translate keep everything on your Mac.

All of these requests go directly from your Mac to the provider. I never see, relay or store them, and there is no proxy in between. What the provider does with the data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy, so please read those for any provider you enable.

3. API keys

Keys you enter for cloud engines and AI providers are stored in the macOS Keychain, Apple's encrypted credential storage. They are sent only to the provider they belong to. They are never sent to me or to anyone else.

4. What stays on your Mac

  • Settings, vocabulary and profiles are stored locally in the app's preferences.
  • Usage counters (number of sessions, words, dictation time, AI-processed dictations and the date you first used the app) are stored locally and drive the Stats screen. They are never transmitted. You can reset them in Settings.
  • Your last dictation. So you can paste again if a paste fails, Sonotexta keeps your most recent transcription on disk for up to 24 hours. You can turn this off, or clear it at any time, under Settings → Privacy. With it off, the text is kept in memory for the current session only.
  • Downloaded models for the on-device engines are stored on your Mac.

5. Smart contact detection

To switch language and tone profiles automatically, Sonotexta can read the title of the window you are typing in (for example the contact name in a messaging app). This uses the macOS Accessibility permission you grant, is processed on your device only, and is never stored or transmitted. Accessibility access is also what lets Sonotexta paste text into other apps.

6. Model downloads

The on-device engines need speech models. Sonotexta downloads them when you choose to use an engine, directly from the model publishers' hosts (for example Hugging Face). Like any download, this reveals your IP address to the host. The models stay on your Mac afterwards.

7. Automatic updates

Sonotexta uses Sparkle to check for new versions. A check fetches a small update feed (appcast.xml) from GitHub Pages, and an update, if you accept it, is downloaded from the same place. GitHub sees the usual details of a web request, such as your IP address and a user agent naming the app and its version, and handles them under its own privacy policy. I do not receive that information.

No system profile is sent. Sparkle can optionally attach anonymous system details (macOS version, CPU, RAM and so on) to update checks. Sonotexta does not turn that on: the app does not set Sparkle's SUEnableSystemProfiling option, which is off by default, so no hardware or system profile data is sent.

8. What the app collects

Nothing. Sonotexta has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no user accounts and no server-side storage. There is nothing to request, export or delete, because the app sends me no data about you.

9. This website

sonotexta.com is a static site with no analytics, no cookies and no tracking scripts. Its only third-party resource is Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces and so receives your IP address when a page loads, under Google's privacy policy. Like any web server, the one hosting this site may keep standard access logs (IP address, requested page, time) for security and troubleshooting. They are not used for analytics.

Links to GitHub, GitHub Pages and Buy Me a Coffee take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. The Download button fetches the app from GitHub Pages.

10. Third-party services

Only when you choose to use them, your data is handled by the service you picked, under its own privacy policy. Sonotexta can work with:

  • OpenAI, Deepgram and ElevenLabs for cloud speech recognition
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter and Ollama Cloud for AI cleanup, and DeepL and OpenAI for translation
  • Hugging Face and other model publishers for model downloads
  • GitHub Pages for update checks and downloads (via Sparkle)

You are responsible for reviewing the privacy policies of any service you enable.

11. Children's privacy

Sonotexta is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the app collects nothing, there is nothing to collect.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date. Because the project is open source, changes to how the app handles data are also visible in the public code history.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to developer@sonotexta.com.
